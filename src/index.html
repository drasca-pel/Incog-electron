Incog Electric Lab: quick guide

Building a circuit

  1. Click a part in Parts, then click the canvas to place it. R rotates it, Enter or Esc stops placing and returns to the arrow.
  2. Connect with Wire (W), or just press on a part's pin and drag. Click to bend, double-click or Esc to finish. Space flips the bend direction.
  3. Always place a GND (0 V reference). Without it the app picks one for you and tells you.
  4. Click a part and change its label and value in the Properties tab. Values accept 4.7k, 10u, 2meg.

Reading answers

  • Nodes are named A, B, C… automatically (rename them in Properties).
  • The Answers tab lists every voltage, current and power. Type in the search box to filter, or type a formula like V(A)-V(B) or I(R1)+I(R2).
  • The Probe tool stamps a live readout on any wire, pin or part. Hover over anything for a quick tooltip.
  • The Theorems tab solves Superposition, Thevenin, Norton, Maximum power, Substitution, Reciprocity and KCL/Tellegen with the working shown.

Sign conventions

For every part, V = V(pin a) − V(pin b) and I flows from pin a to pin b through the part. Pin a is the left pin (or the top pin when rotated once). For a source, a negative power means it is delivering power.
